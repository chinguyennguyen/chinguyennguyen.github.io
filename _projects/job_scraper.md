I'm learning to build things, and AI has been a huge help in removing the coding barrier so I can focus on what actually matters: design and functionality.

I built this job scraper and tracker to make my own job search less chaotic. It automatically pulls fresh listings from LinkedIn and Indeed across multiple cities and search terms, filtering for postings from the last 72 hours. Each job gets scored for relevance based on keyword matching and company priority, so I can see what's worth my time at a glance. A simple local dashboard then lets me track the whole pipeline: weekly picks with application goals, a full tracker of every application and its status, and stats on my search funnel and conversion rates. Everything is configured through a single YAML file, and the whole thing runs in Docker so there's no local Python setup required.
